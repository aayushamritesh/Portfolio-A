M.Tech Thesis

IIIT Delhi Jan 2026 – Present (2025 – 2027) Team Size: 2

Human Heart Developmental Atlas

Advisor: Dr. Vibhor Kumar, Associate Professor, Department of Computational Biology, IIIT Delhi

Research Overview & Aim

During human cardiogenesis, progenitor cells undergo strictly timed lineage bifurcations giving rise to cardiomyocytes, epicardial, endocardial, and vascular lineages. Understanding the molecular underpinnings of heart development is vital for modeling congenital heart diseases and regenerative therapeutics.

This thesis constructs an integrated, multi-resolution Human Heart Developmental Atlas by leveraging massive public and collaborator single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) profiles spanning gestational stages through mature cardiac states.

Technical Innovations & Pipeline

  • Large-Scale Dataset Integration: Constructing scalable computational pipelines utilizing Seurat, Harmony, and BPCells for disk-backed matrix operations, handling hundreds of thousands of cardiac cells without memory bottlenecks.
  • Cell-Type Harmonization & QC: Establishing rigorous quality control, doublet removal, mitochondrial filtering, and unified cell ontology mapping across disparate single-cell studies.
  • Dynamics, Velocity & Spatial Extension: Applying RNA Velocity (spliced vs. unspliced kinetics) and Spatial Transcriptomics to reconstruct cell-state transitions, lineage progression, and developmental dynamics in anatomic context.

ZINB-VAE for scRNA-seq

Zero-Inflated Negative Binomial Autoencoder for Denoising

Implemented a Zero-Inflated Negative Binomial Variational Autoencoder (ZINB-VAE) from scratch in PyTorch with KL divergence annealing to explicitly model count sparsity, dropout events, and overdispersion in scRNA-seq.

Benchmarked against PCA and standard MSE-Autoencoder baselines; ZINB-VAE demonstrated substantially lower expression imputation error and superior preservation of latent cell-type manifold clusters.
